Time Spent Calculator

Upload a PNG screenshot or an Excel sheet with a "Time Spent" column (like 1hr 20m, 40m). You get the total in hours and minutes. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    Screenshots are read with OCR, so check the list above against your image.
